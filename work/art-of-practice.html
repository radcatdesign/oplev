Art of Practice

    
The operating system for the solo clinical professional.

    OpsAgents

The story

      
What it does.

    

    

      
Solo clinical professionals pay for a stack of apps and still can't answer a simple question: am I making money? We designed and built Art of Practice, one platform for the schedule, notes, client files, payments and credentials, plus the investor overview and product demo.

    

  






  

    

      

        
What we built

        
What we built.

      

      
6 parts / working prototype

    

    
    
      
  • platform
    Today view
    Revenue, schedule, open notes, unpaid invoices and license renewals on one screen.
    • 
      
  • tool
    Charting and files
    Note templates, consent forms and client records, private by design.
    • 
      
  • agent
    AI-assisted notes
    Speak the findings, then review a structured note before it's saved.
    • 
      
  • tool
    Practice reports
    One financial picture across every place the practitioner works.
    • 
      
  • tool
    Professional vault
    Licenses, credentials and continuing education, with renewal reminders.
    • 
      
  • deck
    Investor overview
    The story, the product and the plan in twelve slides, with a recorded demo.
