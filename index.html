~/oplev/method

        
How a build runs.

      

      
discuss / build / implement / measure

    

    
    
      
  1. 01
    Discuss
    We interview the people doing the work, with help from Mira, our AI interviewer, and map how it actually gets done.
    2. 
      
  2. 02
    Build
    We write it down and build it into your tools: playbooks, workflows, dashboards, agents and the lead tools on your site.
    3. 
      
  3. 03
    Implement
    We roll it out with your team one function at a time, until it holds without us in the room.
    4. 
      
  4. 04
    Measure
    We score it. The OpLev Score shows how much of the business runs without leaning on one person, and where to build next.

~/oplev/ownership

        
You own everything we build.

      

      
no lock-in

    

    

      
It lives in your accounts.
Docs, workflows and agents run in your tools, under your logins.

      
Agents ask before they act.
Anything that touches a customer, money or a contract waits for a person to approve it.

      
Every action leaves a log.
You can see what each agent did, when it did it and what it was working from.

      
Stay because it works.
After the build, support is month to month. Leave when it stops being useful. The system stays yours.
