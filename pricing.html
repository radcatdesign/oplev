Questions.

        

      

      

        

          Where do we start?
          
With the OpLev Score. Your team answers a short set of questions with Mira, and we show you where the business leans on one person. That tells us which plan fits.

        

        

          Do we even need this?
          
Maybe not. If the business runs fine while you take two weeks off, keep your money. We'll tell you that on the first call.

        

        

          What does the + on Whole company mean?
          
It starts at $15k a month and goes up with team size and the number of functions we take on. We quote it after the OpLev Score, so you know the number before you commit.

        

        

          Can we change plans?
          
Yes. Move up or down at the start of any month.

        

        

          What happens if we stop?
          
Everything stays. The playbooks, workflows and agents live in your accounts, so they keep running without us.
